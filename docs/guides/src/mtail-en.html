SoarF5J for EdgeTX · guide

Installing and setting up an M-tail model

Step by step: from copying the files to the SD card to checking the controls and the first F5J flight. The example is the MTail template: the rudder on CH6 and an elevator in two halves, each with its own servo (CH7 and CH8).

EdgeTX 2.12TX15TX16S MK3TX16S · T16 · T18October 2026

SoarF5J is a single widget for EdgeTX color radios with two roles. The competition screen runs the F5J flight: motor run, glide, finish, start height and the voice countdown of the working time. The setup pages let you set up the model right on the radio, without a computer or Companion. The model template provides the flight modes, timers, logical switches, global variables, curves, mixes and channels, and the widget reads and changes them.

What you need

  • A radio with EdgeTX 2.12 and a color landscape screen of 480×272, 480×320 or 800×480. Tested on the TX15 and TX16S-class radios.
  • A receiver with at least 8 channels.
  • A computer and a USB cable or a card reader.
  • An altitude sensor with telemetry (the Alt sensor) for the start height.
  • Receiver voltage telemetry (RxBt, Cels, A1 or A2) for the low battery warning.
  • Optionally EdgeTX Companion, to try everything in the simulator first.

Safety

  • The setup pages write changes to the model at once and move the control surfaces.
  • Before setup, remove the propeller or disconnect the motor. Do not rely on a switch position alone.
  • Set up a copy of the model or work in the simulator first, then on the radio, with the model held securely.
  • After setup, check the channel order, directions, endpoints and failsafe.

1Update EdgeTX and download the files

  1. Check the EdgeTX version: SYS → the last tab with the i icon, the VERS line. You need EdgeTX 2.12 (the latest is 2.12.5). If your version is older, update the firmware as the EdgeTX manual describes (manual.edgetx.org), for example with EdgeTX Buddy.
  2. Download the official SD card content for EdgeTX 2.12. From the latest 2.12 release at github.com/EdgeTX/edgetx-sdcard/releases (now v2.12.3), take the archive for your radio's screen:
    RadioScreenArchive
    TX16S, TX16S MK2, T16, T18480×272c480x272.zip
    TX15480×320c480x320.zip
    TX16S MK3800×480c800x480.zip
  3. Download the voice pack of the same version from github.com/EdgeTX/edgetx-sdcard-sounds/releases, for example edgetx-sdcard-sounds-en-2.12.3.zip for English. Without it the radio does not speak numbers: time, altitude and voltage.
  4. Open the project page github.com/vitas/soar-edgetx, press Code → Download ZIP and unzip it. The dist/SDCARD folder is not a complete SD card and has no EdgeTX voice pack, so only the three items listed in section 2 are copied from it.

2Copy the files to the SD card

  1. Connect the radio to the computer with a USB cable and choose USB Storage (SD): the card shows up as a drive. You can also take the card out of the radio and put it in a card reader.
  2. Back up the card, at least the MODELS and RADIO folders.
  3. The official content first: unzip the SD card archive and the voice pack to the root of the card and agree to replace the files. Your models and the files you added yourself stay.
  4. Then copy only three items from dist/SDCARD to the root of the card:
    What (from dist/SDCARD)Where on the cardWhat for
    WIDGETS/SoarF5J/WIDGETS/SoarF5Jthe widget: competition and setup pages
    TEMPLATES/3.SoarEdgeTx/TEMPLATES/3.SoarEdgeTxthe MTail, VTail and XTail model templates
    SOUNDS/en/*.wav/SOUNDS/enthe template's voice prompts: launch, landing, motor off and others
  5. Eject the drive safely, unplug the cable and restart the radio.
Updating the widgetBefore copying a new version, delete the whole old /WIDGETS/SoarF5J folder. Otherwise old compiled .luac files may stay on the card.
Radio voice in another language?The template prompts are recorded in English and live in /SOUNDS/en. Copy the same .wav files into the folder of your radio's voice language too, for example /SOUNDS/de, or they may not play.

3Create a model from the template

  1. Open the model list on the radio and choose New model.
  2. Open the 3.SoarEdgeTx template folder and choose the template for your radio:
    RadioTemplateWhat is inside
    RadioMaster TX15tx15-MTailfull set, 14 global variables; CRSF module (ExpressLRS/Crossfire), 16 channels
    RadioMaster TX16S MK3tx16s-mk3-MTailfull set, 14 variables; CRSF, 16 channels
    TX16S, TX16S MkII, T16, T18 and similartx16s-MTailcompatible set, 9 variables; internal multiprotocol module, 8 channels
  3. Rename the model in its settings if you like.
  4. Check the RF module. If you use another radio system, change the module type in the model settings. For ExpressLRS, first do the steps in “ExpressLRS” below. Bind the receiver.
  5. Set the failsafe so that the motor stops when the signal is lost (CH3 at minimum).
The tx16s-MTail template (9 variables)Some parameters are fixed in it, and the setup pages show them as N/A: Elevator Travel, Aileron -> Elevator, Rudder -> Elevator, Flap Differential, Elevator Differential and the amount of thermal camber. The elevator mixes from the ailerons and from the rudder work with a fixed value of 20, and the elevator differential is 50.

ExpressLRS: update and full resolution

  1. Update ExpressLRS in the radio module and in the receiver to the same version 3, preferably the latest stable release. Versions 2 and 3 do not work together. Flash with ExpressLRS Configurator: choose the device, the version and the same Binding Phrase for the module and the receiver. Flash the module over USB (choose USB Serial (VCP) on the radio and the EdgeTX Passthrough method in Configurator) or over WiFi. Flash the receiver over WiFi: power it up without the radio, and after about a minute it opens the ExpressLRS RX network with the password expresslrs. Connect to it and open 10.0.0.1. More at expresslrs.org.
  2. Open SYS → Tools → ExpressLRS and set Packet Rate to “100Hz Full” and Switch Mode to “8ch”. Of the Full modes, 100Hz has the longest range. The script must be the same version as the module.
  3. If the receiver outputs were remapped, set them back to the defaults: output 1 to channel 1 and so on up to output 8 to channel 8.
Why Full modeIn the normal modes (Hybrid, Wide) channel 5 is the arm channel and is sent with only two positions, and channels 6–8 have low resolution. The right flap on CH5 then sits at an end, and the surfaces on CH6–CH8 move in steps. There is no need to move channels to other outputs: Full mode solves it.
Channel 5 stays the arm channelWhen the right flap is above center, ExpressLRS treats the model as armed. If the ExpressLRS script then will not let you change a setting, deploy the brakes (SF↓, throttle stick up): channel 5 goes below center.

The wing and the motor are wired the same way in all template variants (MTail, VTail, XTail); only the tail differs. The template is neutral: centers, travels, reverses and curves are not fitted to any particular glider; that is done in section 6. The alignment curves CV1–CV4 start linear: −100, −50, 0, 50, 100. The brake curves CV5, CV6 and CV14 start as after Reset on page 6: with the brakes off the ailerons (CH1/CH2) sit at 0 and the flaps (CH4/CH5) at −50 and +50 %, so three quarters of the flap travel stays for the brakes.

5Screens and widget controls

The template already has three screens with the SoarF5J widget. Screen 1 is the competition (Page = 1). Screen 2 has six setup tiles (Page = 2…7) in two columns: Switches, Mixes & Battery and Outputs on the left, Wing alignment, Airbrake curves and Aileron/camber on the right. Screen 3 is the Motor page (Page = 8). Switch screens with the PAGE key or by swiping.

How to open a page

  1. Hold your finger on the tile until a menu appears and choose Full screen. If the tiles only get a frame, tap the tile you want once more: then the menu appears. With the keys: hold Enter, choose the tile with the wheel, press Enter and choose Full screen. A plain tap or Enter does not open the page: EdgeTX passes keys and touches to a widget only in full screen.
  2. Pages that move the control surfaces first show a W A R N I N G: “Please disable the motor!”. Press Enter only after the motor is disconnected. The Motor page warns about the propeller (step 6.8).
  3. To exit, tap the X at the top right or long-press RTN. On exit the temporary setup modes turn off.

6Model setup step by step

Do the steps in order. The motor is disconnected (except in step 6.8), and the model is held securely.

6.1. Switches Page 2 · Switches

Each row has a drop-down list with a switch position. Check the assignments and change them if needed.

NONE and ONNONE turns the function off; ON keeps it on all the time, without a switch. In a model made from an older template, a row with no switch shows ON: EdgeTX treats an empty condition as true, so the function was always on. To turn it off, choose NONE.
  • SA has two roles: down arms the motor, up turns on the Aileron -> Elevator mix, and the middle does neither.
  • To switch Rudder -> Elevator by hand, assign it a free switch.
  • A spring-loaded switch works best for Start/Stop timer and Motor. With a normal switch, return SE up right after the flick: SE↓ while the motor runs stops the motor.
  • Altitude callouts work only when both rows are on, the third and the last. By default they are SB↓ and SB↑, which are never on together. To hear the altitude at SB↓, set the Report current altitude every 10 sec. row to ON.

6.2. Channels and directions Page 4 · Outputs

The page shows the named channels. Each row has the channel number, the name, a direction arrow and a bar with three points (lower end, center, upper end) and a live position marker.

  • Direction: select the arrow and press Enter, or tap it: the channel is reversed.
  • Center and endpoints: select the bar and press Enter, then choose what to change: Offset moves all three points, Range changes the travel symmetrically, Lower the lower end, Center the center, Upper the upper end. Change it with Inc/Dec or by dragging the point, and finish with Enter or Exit.
  • Channel number: Enter on the number and Inc/Dec move the channel with its mixes to another output. You need this only if the servos are not wired as in the table in section 4.
ActionCorrect response
Elevator stick backboth elevator halves up
Rudder stick rightrudder right
Aileron stick rightright aileron up, left aileron down

For an M-tail, first make both elevator halves move together: reverse ElevL or ElevR if needed. Then adjust Center, Lower and Upper: at neutral the halves should be level, with equal travel up and down. If the Aileron -> Elevator and Rudder -> Elevator mixes are on, the elevator halves move in opposite directions with the aileron and rudder sticks; that is normal (step 6.6).

6.3. Wing alignment Page 5 · Wing alignment

Aligns the four wing surfaces: Lft ail, Lft flp, Rgt flp, Rgt ail. The changes are written to the curves and to the channel centers and endpoints.

  • The throttle stick selects one of the five curve points.
  • The throttle trim moves the pair of flaps, the elevator trim the pair of ailerons.
  • The rudder trim aligns the left flap with the right one, the aileron trim the left aileron with the right one.
  • The on-screen sliders move single surfaces.
  • Order: the end points first, then the center, then ±50 %.
  • Reset returns the alignment to the start; press it only if you want to start over.

6.4. Airbrakes Page 6 · Airbrake curves

  • The throttle stick selects a curve point, and the surfaces move live.
  • The throttle trim changes the flap point (Flaps), the aileron trim the aileron point (Aileron), and the elevator trim the elevator compensation at this point of the brake travel (Elevator). The on-screen sliders also work. The trims themselves do not move while the page is open.
  • Brake -> Elevator on page 3 sets the amount of compensation, separately for each flight mode, and the Elevator curve sets how it builds up along the brake travel: at the bottom of the plot there is none, on the middle line it equals Brake -> Elevator, and at the top it is twice as much.
  • The last point (brakes off) always stays at the bottom and has no slider, so in normal flight the elevator stays where the trim puts it.
  • Usually, with full brakes the flaps are down and the ailerons up. If the model pitches its nose up or down as the brakes come out, note at what travel it happens and adjust that point of the Elevator curve.
  • Reset returns the curves to their starting shape.
  • Models made from older templates have no CV14 curve: the page shows N/A, the compensation follows the flap curve, and the trims move as usual.

6.5. Ailerons and camber Page 7 · Aileron/camber

  • The page shows four parameters: for each, its code, the trim that changes it and the current value.
  • While the page is open, the trims change the parameters: the aileron trim the aileron travel (Ail), the rudder trim aileron → flap (AiF), the elevator trim camber → aileron (CbA), and the throttle trim the amount of thermal camber (CbX; N/A in tx16s).
  • In flight the camber position comes from the CbP input, by default trim T3 (in Mode 2 usually the throttle trim). After you leave the page, the trims work as usual again.

6.6. M-tail mixes and battery Page 3 · Mixes & Battery

The values are stored separately for each flight mode. First select the mode with its switch, Cruise (the default), Speed (SD↑) or Float (SD↓), then change the values. The current mode shows at the top right, for example FM0:Cruise. While the page is open, the trims also change the mix values.

FieldIn the templateWhen it works
Aileron -> Elevator20 (Cruise)in Cruise, Speed and Float, when the mix switch is on (by default SA↑)
Rudder -> Elevator20 (Cruise)in Cruise, Speed and Float, when the mix switch is on; off with NONE (the default)
Elevator Differential50 (Cruise)together with Aileron -> Elevator

Both mixes move the elevator halves in opposite directions, so the elevator helps the roll. The sign of the value sets the direction, and 0 turns the mix off. Check the values in each mode: Cruise, Speed and Float.

Elevator Differential reduces the travel of the half going down in the Aileron -> Elevator mix: at 50 by half, for example 6° up and 3° down; at 0 the travel is equal, and a negative value reduces the travel up.

Direction check

  1. Turn the mix on with its switch (for Aileron -> Elevator by default SA↑).
  2. Aileron stick right: the right elevator half should go up and the left one down, like the ailerons (Fig. 2). The half going down moves less.
  3. If it is the other way round, change the sign: 20 → −20.
  4. Rudder -> Elevator with the same sign moves the halves with the rudder stick right the same way as the aileron stick right does. If you changed the sign of Aileron -> Elevator, change it here too.
  5. Start with small values and check them in flight at a safe height.
Rudder -> Elevator is off by defaultIn the template this mix has no switch (NONE), so it does not work even though the value 20 is set. To switch it, assign a switch on the Switches page (step 6.1); ON keeps the mix on all the time.
The tx16s-MTail templateHere both fields and Elevator Differential show N/A: the amount is set directly in the CH7 and CH8 mixes, the AilEle and RudEle lines with weight +20 on CH7 and −20 on CH8. To change the direction, swap the signs in these lines in the model's mixer menu. To turn a mix off, set its weight to 0. The differential is set in the AilEle lines: Diff −50 on both CH7 and CH8. Change it the same way in both lines.

Battery warning level (V) is the receiver battery voltage threshold. Below it the radio vibrates and says the voltage, at most once every 20 seconds.

6.7. Motor check propeller off!

  1. Open the radio's channel monitor.
  2. Without arming (SA not down), CH3 stays at −100 at any throttle stick position.
  3. Arm with SA↓. Start with SE↓ and right back: the motor starts when the switch returns. The Motor flight mode comes on, and CH3 follows the throttle stick.
  4. Find the stick position where CH3 = +100 (full throttle). The motor input in the template is reversed (I4:Mot: source Thr, weight −100). At the start the motor gets the throttle that matches the stick position right away.
  5. Stopping: SE↓ again, moving SA out of the down position, or automatically after 30 seconds of motor run.
  6. Only after this check, connect the motor and fit the propeller.

6.8. Motor idle Page 8 · Motor

The idle is the motor throttle at minimum stick in the Motor mode. Set it by ear with the motor running: at minimum the motor should turn slowly but steadily. This is the only step with the motor connected and the propeller on: hold the model and do not stand in front of the propeller.

  1. Open screen 3, the Motor tile in full screen, and confirm the warning with Enter. The page itself does not start the motor.
  2. Throttle stick at minimum. Start the motor as usual: SA↓, then SE↓ and right back. FM2:Motor appears in the title.
  3. Throttle trim (T3) up or down: each click changes the idle by 1, holding repeats, and the motor changes speed at once.
  4. Stop the motor as usual and close the page. The value stays in the model.
  • Range 0…60, default 37. At 0 the motor stops at minimum throttle.
  • The value is the first point of the CV13 Pot curve. The page keeps the middle point at half the value; full throttle does not change.
  • While the page is open, the throttle trim does not move the camber. In a model made from an older template (without L49 and SF39) the trim works as usual and does not change the idle: select the value and turn the wheel, or touch it and drag your finger.

7Flying F5J with the widget

Screen 1 (Page = 1) shows the flight state, the working time, the motor time and the start height.

Ready→ Motor ON→ Glide→ Finished or Zero result
  1. Ready: the screen shows the working time from timer 1, 10:00 by default. To change it, open the widget in full screen and press Inc/Dec (1 minute per step), or change Start of timer 1 in the model settings.
  2. SA↓ arms: the flight, the timers and the height are reset.
  3. SE↓ and back starts the motor: state Motor ON. The voice calls 10 and 15 seconds of motor run and counts 10…1 from the 20th second to 30 seconds.
  4. The motor stops (SE↓, SA out of the down position, or automatically at 30 seconds): state Glide. The start height (the Alt+ sensor) is captured while the motor runs and for 10 more seconds after it stops.
  5. Move SA up (SA↑) right away: this disarms and turns on the Aileron -> Elevator mix. While SA is down, the next SE flick starts the motor again.
  6. In the glide the voice calls the remaining working time: every minute while more than 2 minutes remain; every 15 seconds from 2 to 1 minute; every 5 seconds from 1 minute to 10 seconds; then every second 10…1.
  7. Landing: SF↓ enters landing mode, and the throttle stick deploys the brakes; SF↑ leaves it.
  8. Finish: an SE flick (SA not down) gives the state Finished and stops the time. With the widget in full screen, Enter or a tap also works. The next press resets to Ready for a new flight.
Zero resultRestarting the motor in the glide gives a Zero result, as in the F5J rules. Do not move SA down during the flight: that arms again and resets the flight.

The widget does not keep a flight log.

9Checklist before the first flight

  • All control surfaces move the right way in Cruise, Speed and Float.
  • The elevator halves are level at neutral and have equal travel.
  • The Aileron -> Elevator and Rudder -> Elevator mixes help the roll in the right direction with small values; Rudder -> Elevator is on deliberately or set to 0.
  • Without arming, CH3 is at minimum; the failsafe stops the motor.
  • The motor stops with an SE flick and automatically after 30 seconds.
  • Landing mode (SF↓): flaps down, ailerons up, and the throttle stick controls the brakes smoothly.
  • The altitude and receiver voltage telemetry show, and the voice prompts are heard.
  • A range check is done.
